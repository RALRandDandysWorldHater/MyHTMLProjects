🎮 How to Play

BUILD: Select a block above and click the grid.
MOVE: Use the LEFT and RIGHT arrow keys.
JUMP: Press SPACE or the UP arrow.
COLLECT: Touch the coins to collect them.
WIN: Reach the finish flag on the right side.
EDIT: Return to the editor to change your level.

Tip: Place ground underneath Mario so he doesn't fall!
